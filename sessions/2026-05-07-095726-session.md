## Major decisions / context worth remembering

- **`.blend` files can't load directly in Three.js.** Three Polyhaven boulder packs were `.blend` only; we shipped procedural icosahedron-displaced shapes and reused the diffuse JPGs as the surface material. If exact silhouettes wanted later: re-download Polyhaven assets as `.glb` from the same download page, drop in `public/assets/models/`, swap the procedural geometry call in `boulders.ts` for GLTFLoader. Placement logic stays.
- **Texture footprint in `public/assets/textures/` is now ~91 MB**: forest diffuse 9.6 + forest rough 2.9 + river-rocks 13 + boulder diffuses (rock_moss 2.4 + namaqualand_02 14 + namaqualand_03 12). Heavy first-load. Easy fix later: downsize to 2K (~25 MB savings) without losing meaningful detail.
- **No real-shape player NPC** for Mae or Pete — both are wooden-post / bedroll patterns with their identity supplied by the dialogue speaker name. Same constraint as the existing vendors.
- **Audio is procedural** (Web Audio synthesis). Stream ambient + UI tones only. No music yet — locked design wants fingerpicked guitar (commission item).
- **Player-follow shadow camera** keeps shadow render scoped to a 120×120m square around the player. Sun direction captured BEFORE offset so terrain / grass / water lambert math stays correct against absolute world-space sun direction.

## Known limits / heads-up for next session

1. **Texture asset footprint** — 91 MB in `public/`; first-load on a cold browser can be sluggish. Downsize to 2K is a quick win.
2. **Cave site has no 12h respawn** — stream sites do, the mine cave's hidden site is permanent.
3. **No music** — fingerpicked guitar is locked design, commission item.
4. **No footstep / panning splash audio** — the Web Audio system is in place; just needs per-event triggers.
5. **Boulders have no physics colliders** — visual only, walking through them is possible.
6. **Cave darkness override missing** — interior reads as dim from the dark stone material but isn't pitch black; headlamp doesn't get a special chance to shine inside.
7. **Shadow camera follows player but stays 60m half-extent** — far-away mine + town buildings won't shadow each other.
8. **`grass-cover` waits for scatter promises before placing** — there's a 3–5 second window after world load where the bare ground is briefly visible before the cover fills in.
