# Art credits

The hills, the creek, and the town are one painted Sierra palette. Photo
textures stay out of that picture. Where a real asset is the better
surface, it is CC0 and it is listed here.

## Quaternius — Ultimate Nature / megakit

CC0. Already in the game as trees, grass clumps, rocks, and leaf cards
under `public/assets/megakit/`.

The north drift's square-set posts, caps, wedges, and lagging use
`Bark_NormalTree.png` and `Bark_NormalTree_Normal.png` from that pack.
The bark is the same paint as the pines, so a hewn timber reads as part
of the forest instead of a photograph of lumber.

<https://quaternius.com/>

## Kenney

CC0 prototype and furniture kits were considered for the mill and the
shed. The town already has a hand-painted board texture (`innTimber` in
`src/render/painterly.ts`). The stamp-mill walls and the fire-shed boards
use that, so a Kenney plank was not mixed in.

<https://kenney.nl/>

## Poly Haven and ambientCG

CC0. Boulder and ground photographs live in `public/assets/textures/`
(`namaqualand_boulder_*`, `forrest_ground_01_*`, `river_small_rocks_*`,
`rock_moss_set_01_*`). They are not on the drift floor or the mill. A
photo of leaves on the muck fought the lantern-lit interior, so the muck
is a painted tile in the same family as the tunnel rock, and the puddles
are a dark metal so the lantern has something to catch.

<https://polyhaven.com/>

<https://ambientcg.com/>

## Characters

The prospector and the crew are the ranger already in
`public/assets/characters/`. This pass does not replace that mesh. Silas
swings the shovel that mesh already carries.
